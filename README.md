# AI Teaching Board: Instructor Dashboard

One page, no login, no landing page. Open `index.html` and you are on the board.

## Start teaching
1. Double-click `index.html` (Chrome or Edge). No internet or server is needed.
2. Share your screen and press **F** for full screen. Press **H** to hide the toolbar and sidebar, so the picture fills the whole screen.
3. Pick a lesson in the left sidebar. It opens on the **whole picture**.
4. Press **→** (or the clicker) to go to step 1. The board zooms into that step so it fills the screen, big and clear, while its animations keep playing. Press **→** / **←** for the next or previous step, and **O** to go back to the whole picture.
   - Steps never change by themselves. They only move when you press a key or a button.
   - Double-click any card to make it fill the screen. Double-click again to go back to the step.
   - Switching lessons never leaves full screen, so your screen share is not interrupted.

## Board tools
- **Drawing:** pen, highlighter, eraser, arrow, box, text, 7 colours, brush size, undo/redo, clear, download image.
- **Pointing:** laser pointer and spotlight.
- **View:**
  - zoom in and out (buttons, mouse wheel, pinch), pan, fit
  - focus mode with **H**, which hides the toolbar and sidebar
  - collapse the sidebar with **\\**
- **Lesson control:**
  - whole picture, previous/next step and a step menu to jump to any step
  - pause/play the animations
  - a presentation clicker (Page Up/Down) moves between steps
- **Extra board:** blank board (whiteboard, grid, chalkboard, dark).
- **Script panel:** English and Roman Urdu scripts, teleprompter scroll, and pop-out notes for a second screen.

Press `?` for all shortcuts. Drawings are saved in your browser for each lesson.
